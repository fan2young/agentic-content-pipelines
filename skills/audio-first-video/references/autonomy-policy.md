# Autonomy Policy

The workflow has five human decisions:

1. **Content lock:** approve the distribution strategy, selected hook, and exact narration together. This decides what will be claimed.
2. **Voice lock:** approve pronunciation, voice, pace, and the exact audio candidate. This makes audio the timing authority.
3. **Visual direction lock:** approve exactly three static style frames after comparison with the bounded latest-three channel reference pack. A model cannot approve this gate.
4. **Final release lock:** approve the visual proof, final-grade review candidate, recommended/alternative video titles, and shared publishing package. Promotion reuses the approved bytes and does not ask for a second decision.
5. **WeChat branch start:** only after the approved video final exists, ask whether to produce the article edit, article-title options, 2.35:1 cover, and inline images together. Do not infer this approval from the video release lock.

The model owns deterministic intermediate work: schema checks, file presence, fingerprints, clean alignment adjudication, timing construction, runtime and delivery preflights, package validation, public-surface scanning, asset inventory, and closeout of declined optional branches. Ask only about actionable exceptions, not passing checks.

No model approval may change factual claims, narration wording, locked audio bytes, or the final released video without returning to the relevant human checkpoint.
